The Sixth MODE Workshop on Differentiable Programming for experiment design will take place at OAC (Kolymbari, Crete) on **Sept 1–7, 2026**. Mark the date! Registration and abstract submission are open [https://indico.cern.ch/event/1655754/](https://indico.cern.ch/event/1655754/). The MODE workshop is about the codesign and optimization of complex instrumental apparata for fundamental physics, and PHINDER stemmed in part from MODE activities, making the workshop the ideal venue for PHINDER scientists to showcase their early work and plans.
