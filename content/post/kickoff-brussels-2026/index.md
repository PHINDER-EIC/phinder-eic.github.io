Pathfinder Open EIC projects are designed to have periodic in-person meetings, where the governing body of the consortium meets and where all researchers get together to discuss research so far and the next steps. The PHINDER team will meet in Brussels on June 5, at the HPE partner node, and will visit the TU/e partner node laboratory on June 4!
