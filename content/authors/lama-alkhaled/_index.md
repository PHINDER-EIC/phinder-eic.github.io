Lama Alkhaled is PHINDER’s project manager at Luleå University of Technology, in the Department of Computer Science, Electrical and Space Engineering. She is the consortium’s contact for project-management matters and access to internal collaboration resources.

Her work also includes project management in AI-related collaborations, as described by ProcessIT Innovations. Within PHINDER, she supports coordination across the partner institutions alongside project coordinator Fredrik Sandin.
