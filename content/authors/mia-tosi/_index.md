Mia Tosi is an experimental particle physicist at the University of Padova, a member of the CMS experiment at CERN and a founding member of the MODE Collaboration. Her research includes silicon tracking detectors, reconstruction algorithms, online event selection and machine learning for the optimisation of physics measurements. This background connects with PHINDER’s particle-physics applications.
