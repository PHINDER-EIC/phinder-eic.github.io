Magnus Borgström is Professor of Solid State Physics at Lund University and a member of NanoLund. His research focuses on epitaxial crystal growth and semiconductor nanowires, including nanowire solar cells and light-emitting diodes. This expertise is relevant to the materials and optoelectronic components underpinning PHINDER.
