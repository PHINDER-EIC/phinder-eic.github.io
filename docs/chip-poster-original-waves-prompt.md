# Landing-page poster chip with optical-wave background

## Source and method

- **Chip artwork:** the original transparent PNG embedded as `ppt/media/image3.png` in `/home/fs/Downloads/PHINDER_rollup_v1.pptx`.
- **Background reference:** the poster background embedded as `ppt/media/image1.jpeg` in the same presentation.
- **Composition:** the chip artwork was resized proportionally and alpha-composited without generative editing over the new background. The final 1536 × 1024 WebP is `static/media/chip-poster-original-waves.webp`.
- **Generation mode:** image generation was used only for the background. The chip, its device geometry and its internal details are the poster original.
