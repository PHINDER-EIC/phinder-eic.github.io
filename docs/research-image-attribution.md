# Image attribution and reuse notes

Every image published on the PHINDER site should have a visible short credit and a linked full citation. The files below preserve the scientific content; some were cropped only to remove journal page furniture.

## Web-ready assets

### `nature_2026_nanowire_neuron_micrograph.png`

- Provenance: supplied by Anders Mikkelsen’s team for use on the PHINDER homepage.
- Related publication: J. E. Sestoft et al., “Nanoscale photonic artificial neuron with biological signal processing,” *Nature Communications* 17, 4798 (2026).
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-71446-4
- Modification: none; the packaged PNG is byte-identical to the supplied image.
- Required credit: “Micrograph courtesy of Anders Mikkelsen and colleagues. Related publication: Sestoft et al., Nature Communications 17, 4798 (2026).”
- Recommended use: primary Science image or a portrait-oriented hero image. Preserve the labels and 1 µm scale bar; use `object-fit: contain` rather than cropping.
- Explanation: the false-colour electron micrograph shows InP nanowire LEDs and photodiodes connected through a common gate to an InAs nanowire field-effect transistor.
